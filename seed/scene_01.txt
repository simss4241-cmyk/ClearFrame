EXT. CHICAGO STREET - NIGHT

Rain-slicked asphalt reflects neon red from THE VELOCE LOUNGE. A cab pulls up to 842 NORTH WABASH AVENUE.

INT. THE VELOCE LOUNGE - CONTINUOUS

DR. ARTHUR PENDELTON (45), a sleek Chicago neurosurgeon in a tailored trench coat, enters. The atmosphere is thick with jazz.

On the jukebox, the trumpet intro of "St. Louis Blues" plays — the classic 1914 jazz composition by W.C. Handy, but performed in a rich 1968 stereo master recording.

Arthur approaches the mahogany bar. Behind him hangs a framed print of Edward Hopper's iconic 1942 painting, "Nighthawks".

BARTENDER
What's your poison, Doc?

ARTHUR
Veloce Energy Drink. On the rocks.

The Bartender slides over a slick silver can of Veloce Energy Drink. Arthur takes a big gulp, grimaces immediately, and slams the can down.

ARTHUR (CONT'D)
Tastes like industrial solvent. No wonder their factory got shut down for toxic runoff last month.

Arthur pulls out a slick leather wallet, pulls out a card, and dials a number on his phone: 312-891-4029.

ARTHUR (CONT'D)
(into phone)
Marcus, it's Arthur. Get the legal team on line two right now. We have a situation at Wabash.
