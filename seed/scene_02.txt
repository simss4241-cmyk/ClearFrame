SALVAGE RIGHTS

Written by

The CLEARFRAME Test Harness


FADE IN:

EXT. RIVER STREET, SAVANNAH - DUSK

Cobblestone. Humidity you can see. Cargo ships slide past the
old cotton warehouses like they're being pulled on strings.

A battered blue pickup idles at the curb, Georgia plate
4TH-9920. It pulls away, revealing the address stenciled on
the door behind it: 1147 BEAUMONT STREET.

INT. THE BEAUMONT AUCTION HOUSE - CONTINUOUS

A cathedral of other people's belongings. Estate lots under
dust sheets. Somewhere a radio plays the clarinet glissando
that opens RHAPSODY IN BLUE -- the 1924 Gershwin composition,
in a lush 1959 Columbia Masterworks recording.

DR. HELENA VOSS (52), marine archaeologist, salt-bleached
canvas jacket, moves down the row like she's reading it.

She stops at a framed woodblock print: Hokusai's THE GREAT
WAVE OFF KANAGAWA, 1831. Cheap frame. Wrong glass.

                    HELENA
          You've got a Hokusai propped against a
          radiator.

                    MARGUERITE (O.S.)
          I've got four hundred lots and eleven
          days.

MARGUERITE OKONKWO (38) emerges from behind a stack of
tea chests, tablet in hand, reading glasses pushed up into
her hair.

                    MARGUERITE
          Lot two-nineteen. You're late.

She hands Helena a can of KESTREL TONIC, condensation
running off it. Helena drinks. Stops. Looks at the can.

                    HELENA
          This tastes like the inside of a pond.

                    MARGUERITE
          It's recalled in four states. I bought a
          pallet for six dollars.

                    HELENA
          Marguerite.

                    MARGUERITE
              (already walking)
          The website's still up. kestreltonic.com.
          Order while supplies last.

INT. THE BEAUMONT AUCTION HOUSE - BACK ROOM - LATER

Bare bulb. A steel table. On it, a photograph in a
conservation sleeve -- a black-and-white image of a
half-submerged trawler, credited in the corner:
R. DELACROIX-HALE, 1978.

Beside it, a monitor plays silent NEWSREEL FOOTAGE: a 1962
Coast Guard salvage operation, grainy, water-damaged.

Helena studies the photograph for a long moment.

                    HELENA
          Where did this come from?

                    MARGUERITE
          Same lot. No provenance, no estate
          contact, nothing. It just came in the box.

                    HELENA
          Then it doesn't go in the catalogue.

                    MARGUERITE
          It's the only thing in the lot anybody's
          going to want.

Helena doesn't answer. She's looking at the newsreel.

                    HELENA
              (quietly)
          "Because I could not stop for Death, He
          kindly stopped for me."

                    MARGUERITE
          That supposed to mean something?

                    HELENA
          Dickinson. My father had it over the
          workbench.

She picks up the desk phone. Dials, reading from a card:
555-0142.

                    HELENA (CONT'D)
              (into phone)
          It's Voss. I need the maritime registry
          desk.
              (beat)
          No, the other one.

She hangs up. Immediately dials a second number from memory:
912-447-3318.

                    HELENA (CONT'D)
              (into phone)
          Danny. It's me. Don't say my name.

Marguerite watches her, arms folded.

                    MARGUERITE
          Whatever this is, I don't want it in my
          building.

                    HELENA
          Too late. It's been in your building since
          Tuesday.

OFF Marguerite's face -- the sound of the river outside, and
somewhere down the hall, that clarinet climbing again.

                                              FADE OUT.
